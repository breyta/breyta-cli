# Third-Party Notices

`breyta-cli` is published under the MIT License. The CLI and release artifacts
also rely on third-party open source components.

Release archives do not copy this source-tree summary. Their notice is
generated independently for each target from the module versions and checksums
embedded in that exact compiled Go binary, and the corresponding full license
texts are retained under `licenses/go/`. This avoids reporting build-only or
platform-specific dependencies that are absent from an artifact.

Release archives also contain `eraserhd/parinfer-rust` v0.4.3 at commit
`7b67f166e8ad12899903a0ea451333c41ed80544` under the ISC license.

The pinned upstream `Cargo.toml` and `Cargo.lock`, complete locked Cargo
dependency inventory, source and binary hashes, ISC license, and Cargo notices
are shipped in every release archive.

Every release archive has a sibling SPDX JSON SBOM generated by pinned Syft
from the final archive, including the bundled `parinfer-rust` executable.
